Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-tuan-muda-one-word-for-your-wi-fi-a1b4fd72) bounty **One Word for Your Wi-Fi** ($2 prize, winner takes all).

The entry is a single word describing my Wi-Fi, aiming to be both the funniest and the most accurate:

> **Situationship**

Wi-Fi that never commits. Full bars one second, gone the next, back again before you can complain — present enough to keep you, absent enough to drive you mad, never once settling down.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
